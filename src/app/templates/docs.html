Known limits

  • Rain amount and T2M_MAX are not used in production.
  • Rain occurrence and T2M_MIN are active only at horizon 1 day.
  • AOD is kept in the master table but not used as a model feature.
  • OpenAQ is excluded from the master table.

Attribution

This project uses publicly accessible data from NASA POWER, CHIRPS, INEGI, CENAPRED and MAIAC. It is not endorsed by any of these providers.
